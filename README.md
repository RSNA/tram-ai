# TRAM-AI

**TRAM-AI** (Transparency in Reporting of Medical Imaging Datasets for Artificial Intelligence) is a consensus-based checklist for verifying whether medical imaging datasets described as publicly available are *functionally* accessible and usable for artificial intelligence (AI) development. Existing reporting guidelines such as CLAIM, CONSORT-AI, and TRIPOD+AI address study design and methodology; TRAM-AI complements them by auditing the dataset itself: whether another researcher can actually find it, download it, interpret it, and reuse it. The framework is aligned with the FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable).

The checklist was developed through a modified Delphi process with an international panel of 33 experts, pilot-validated on 10 published datasets, and reviewed by an advisory group from Latin America and sub-Saharan Africa for cross-regional applicability. The final checklist has 14 items in four domains: **Dataset Access and Persistence** (3 items), **Technical Interoperability** (4 items), **Metadata and Reproducibility** (4 items), and **Ethical, Legal, and Maintenance** considerations (3 items).
